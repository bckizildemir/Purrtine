About the app
Do I need an account?
No. Your cats, tasks, history and photos stay on your iPhone. There are no ads and no tracking.
Do you send what I type somewhere?
Only if you allow it. I try to understand you on your iPhone first. For a hard sentence I can ask a server for help, but only after you say yes, and you can turn that off in Settings.
Can my partner use it too?
Yes. Add the people in your home, and each completed task shows who did it.
When can I get it?
Soon on the App Store, for iPhone with iOS 18.4 or later, in English and Turkish.
