CatCareCalendar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

CatCareCalendar is a local-first app. Your data belongs to you and stays on your device unless you explicitly use a feature that requires processing elsewhere. This policy explains exactly what that means.

Data stored on your device

All cats, care tasks, schedules, completion history, caregiver names, and photos you add are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData) and the app's private storage. We do not run user accounts, and we cannot see this data.

What leaves your device

  • Task Assistant (optional, opt-in). If you give consent and the on-device interpreter cannot understand a request, the request text, task titles, task status/category information, cat names, and random record identifiers needed to interpret it are sent to Google Firebase Cloud Functions in the EU (europe-west1). Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous user identifier only when this opted-in cloud feature is first used, and Apple App Attest helps verify that requests come from the genuine app. Our function forwards the interpretation prompt to Groq, whose processing infrastructure is in the United States. Groq does not use inputs or outputs to train models. It does not retain inference content by default, but may retain it for up to 30 days when needed to investigate abuse or service-reliability problems. Groq also retains non-content service usage metadata. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or tracking.
  • Dictation. Voice input uses Apple's Speech Recognition. Audio may be processed by Apple per Apple's own privacy terms; the app only receives the transcribed text.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase: anonymous authentication, app integrity verification, and the EU-hosted server function used by the optional Task Assistant. Firebase Authentication stores an anonymous user identifier and may retain authentication IP logs for a limited period under Google's retention practices.
  • Groq: AI inference for unresolved Task Assistant requests. Groq processes the prompt in the United States and may use subprocessors described in its published service documentation.
  • Apple: speech recognition when you actively use dictation, plus App Attest for app-integrity verification.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad SDKs.
  • No behavioral analytics or tracking SDKs. Firebase Analytics is disabled; Firebase services may collect limited diagnostic and service metadata as described above.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No named user accounts — Firebase sign-in is anonymous and exists only to authenticate opted-in assistant requests.

Notifications

Task reminders are local notifications scheduled entirely on your device. No push notification server is involved.

Photos, camera, and microphone

Cat photos you pick or capture are stored only on your device. Camera, photo picker, microphone, and speech recognition are used only when you actively invoke those features, and each requires your permission through iOS.

Data deletion

Deleting a cat or task removes its data (including photos) from your device. Deleting the app removes its on-device data. You can revoke future cloud processing at any time under Settings → Task Assistant. Cloud inference content is not stored by us as a user history and is not used for model training; Groq's limited temporary retention is described above. To request deletion of an anonymous Firebase identifier or ask a privacy question, email the address below. We will verify and process applicable requests with our service providers.

Children

CatCareCalendar is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
