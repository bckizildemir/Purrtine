CatCareCalendar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

CatCareCalendar is a local-first app. Your data belongs to you and stays on your device unless you explicitly use a feature that requires processing elsewhere. This policy explains exactly what that means.

Data stored on your device

All cats, care tasks, schedules, completion history, caregiver names, and photos you add are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData) and the app's private storage. We do not run user accounts, and we cannot see this data.

What leaves your device

  • Task Assistant (optional, opt-in). If you enable the cloud tier of the Task Assistant in Settings and give consent, the text you type or dictate — together with the minimal task/cat context needed to interpret it — is sent to our backend (Google Firebase Cloud Functions, hosted in the EU, region europe-west1) to be interpreted into a task action. The request is authenticated with an anonymous Firebase session and device integrity attestation (Apple App Attest). We do not use this data for advertising or profiling. If you never grant consent, nothing is sent.
  • Dictation. Voice input uses Apple's Speech Recognition. Audio may be processed by Apple per Apple's own privacy terms; the app only receives the transcribed text.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs (Firebase Analytics is disabled).
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No user accounts — Firebase sign-in is anonymous and exists only to authenticate optional assistant requests.

Notifications

Task reminders are local notifications scheduled entirely on your device. No push notification server is involved.

Photos, camera, and microphone

Cat photos you pick or capture are stored only on your device. Camera, photo picker, microphone, and speech recognition are used only when you actively invoke those features, and each requires your permission through iOS.

Data deletion

Deleting a cat or task removes its data (including photos) from your device. Deleting the app removes all app data. Because we hold no copy of your care data, there is nothing further for us to delete on our side; anonymous assistant requests are not retained as user-identifiable records.

Children

CatCareCalendar is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes
